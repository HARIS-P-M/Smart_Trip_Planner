## visa-singapore | https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore
Singapore: Indian passport holders need a visa for most visits and must apply through an authorised visa agent or the ICA website. Complete the SG Arrival Card online within 3 days before arrival. Passport must be valid 6+ months.
## visa-thailand | https://www.thaievisa.go.th
Thailand: Indian passport holders have been granted visa-free stays of up to 60 days under recent policy, but rules change often. Verify on the Thai e-Visa site and carry proof of onward travel and funds. Passport must be valid 6+ months.
## visa-india | https://www.mea.gov.in
Domestic Destinations (Munnar, Goa): Indian citizens need no visa for domestic travel within India. Carry a government photo ID for hotel check-in and domestic flights.
## visa-japan | https://www.evisa.mofa.go.jp
Japan: Indian passport holders can apply for an e-Visa for short-term tourist visits or through authorized visa processing centres (VFS). Passport must be valid for at least 6 months.
## visa-uae | https://smartservices.icp.gov.ae
Dubai (UAE): Indian passport holders can obtain an e-Visa online or Visa on Arrival if holding a valid US/UK/EU visa or green card. Passport must be valid for 6+ months.
## visa-france | https://france-visas.gouv.fr
Paris (France): Indian passport holders require a Schengen Visa (France). Apply via VFS Global at least 3 weeks prior with proof of accommodation, return flights, and travel insurance.
## visa-indonesia | https://molina.imigrasi.go.id
Bali (Indonesia): Indian passport holders can obtain an e-VOA (e-Visa on Arrival) online or VOA on arrival at Bali airport for 30 days. Passport must be valid for 6+ months.
## insurance | https://www.irdai.gov.in
Travel insurance basics: cover medical emergencies, trip cancellation, baggage loss and delays. Compare sum insured, exclusions (adventure sports, pre-existing conditions) and claim process before buying.
## guide-singapore | https://www.visitsingapore.com
Singapore guide: hot and humid all year; December is the wet monsoon season with afternoon showers, so keep indoor options ready. MRT is the cheapest way to travel; an EZ-Link card works on trains and buses. Hawker centres give cheap, excellent meals.
## guide-bangkok | https://www.tourismthailand.org
Bangkok guide: Nov-Feb is cool and dry, the best season. Dress modestly at temples. BTS Skytrain and river boats avoid road traffic. Street food is inexpensive.
## guide-munnar | https://www.keralatourism.org
Munnar guide: cool hill station, mist and rain are common June-Sept. Roads are winding; hire a local driver. Eravikulam National Park closes in Feb-March for calving season.
## guide-tokyo | https://www.japan.travel
Tokyo guide: Spring (cherry blossoms) and Autumn are peak seasons. Transport is effortless with Suica/Pasmo IC cards on JR and Metro lines. Keep cash for traditional shops and local ramen shops.
## guide-dubai | https://www.visitdubai.com
Dubai guide: Best visited Nov-March when weather is pleasant. Metro and taxis are convenient. Dress modestly when visiting cultural sites and mosques.
## guide-paris | https://www.parisinfo.com
Paris guide: Best visited April-June and Sept-Oct. Use Navigo metro passes. Mind your belongings at major tourist spots like Eiffel Tower and Louvre.
## guide-bali | https://www.indonesia.travel
Bali guide: Dry season is April-October. Hiring a local driver or renting a scooter is ideal. Carry cash (IDR) for local markets and beach warungs.
## guide-goa | https://www.goatourism.gov.in
Goa guide: Domestic destination in India. Peak season is Nov-Feb for beach sports and nightlife. Renting a scooter or car is the most popular way to explore North & South Goa.
